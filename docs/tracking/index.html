Tracking rules

Every published game and player projection is archived, including unpriced projections. A tracked wager is a paper selection at its first observed DraftKings number; side, line and price stay frozen. Official football weeks apply to NFL/CFB; NBA/CBB use Monday–Sunday Eastern. The weekly featured cohort has at most 20 entries, locked before kickoff; later candidates fill empty slots. All remaining selections stay in the full record. Ranking uses absolute model-minus-line disagreement divided by the larger of the model number, line magnitude, and one; moneylines use model probability relative to price break-even. This is a tracking rank, not a calibrated edge. CLV uses a named provider close, or an explicitly labeled last observed prestart quote within 60 minutes. Missing close and missing prices stay missing. Moneyline CLV is in implied-probability points; line-market CLV is in points from the selected side. Returns risk one unit and use the actual observed price. No history is truncated, no historical selections are manufactured, and no real wagers are placed.
